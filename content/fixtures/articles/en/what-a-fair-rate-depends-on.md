# What a fair rate actually depends on

A rate is a story about risk, time and materials. Once you can see the six variables inside a quote, comparing two quotes stops being guesswork.

## What moves a quote?

Time on the job, the number of deliverables, the revision loop, travel and access, the usage rights, and how soon it is needed.

## How do you sanity-check a quote?

1. Ask for the quote broken into those six lines.
2. Compare the lines, not the totals.
3. Remove what you do not need and re-quote.

## What it costs and what it takes

Comparisons take an hour when the quotes are itemised, and a day when they are not.

## FAQ

### Is the cheapest quote a red flag?

Not by itself. Ask what it excludes before deciding.

### Should I share my budget?

A range is usually enough to keep the conversation honest.
