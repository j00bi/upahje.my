# The SST threshold is per category, not one number

**RM500,000** is the registration threshold for information technology, management and professional consultancy, repair and maintenance, and advertising. Food and beverage is **RM1,500,000**. Finance and rental or leasing moved to **RM1,000,000** on 1 July 2025.

Those five numbers come from different rows of one document, and that is the point: there is no single SST threshold to look up. The figure sits in column (3) of the First Schedule to the Service Tax Regulations 2018, per Group and per item, and it changes when an amendment order says it does.

## The number is in the Regulations, not in the guide

Where this goes wrong in practice is that people look for the threshold in the industry guide for their sector, and several guides never print it. The **Guide on Advertising Services** says only that a provider "reaching prescribed threshold of taxable services is required to be registered". So does the Guide on Professional Services. Both are correct and both are useless for the one question the reader has.

The number is in the Regulations. The guides apply it, sometimes in an example instead of a statement. The Management Services guide does this well: *"Z Sdn Bhd is liable to register for service tax in May 2021 because the value of management services provided has exceeded RM500,000.00."*

The information technology guide is blunter, and its Group G wording is the sentence to read carefully: a taxable person with a total value of taxable services, *"whether combined or singly"*, of more than RM500,000 is liable to register. Combined means a consultancy that also does training adds both together against one threshold, rather than testing each service separately.

## What each category actually pays

| Category | Threshold | Where it is printed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT and software consultancy | RM500,000 | IT guide V7, para 8; Group G |
| Management consultancy | RM500,000 | Management guide V5, para 19 and Example 12 |
| Consultancy, training, mentoring | RM500,000 | Consultancy guide, para 24 and FAQ 4 |
| Repair and maintenance | RM500,000 | Repair guide, para 24 and FAQ 2 |
| Advertising | RM500,000 | First Schedule, Group I item 8 |
| Motor vehicle repair | RM500,000 | First Schedule, Group I item 5 |
| Food and beverage | RM1,500,000 | First Schedule, Group B |
| Finance, rental and leasing | RM1,000,000 from 1 July 2025 | P.U.(A) 201/2025, reg. 3 |

**One coincidence worth naming.** RM500,000 is also the sales tax registration figure. The agreement is not a carry-over from one tax to another; we read this figure independently off the service tax First Schedule and off three service tax guides dated 2024 to 2026. That matters because the two taxes test different things, and a business that only sells services is not registering for sales tax at all.

## The deadline is the month after you cross

From the repair and maintenance guide: if the total value of taxable services exceeds RM500,000, the application to register must be made **no later than the last day of the following month**.

That makes the crossing date, not the threshold, the number to keep. A firm that crosses in March registers by the end of April. A firm that crosses in March and notices in June has missed it, and the invoices raised in between are the problem, not the registration.

The measurement itself runs on the eleven-month window we set out in [how the threshold is measured](https://upahje.my/en/articles/sst-threshold-eleven-months): this month plus the eleven before it, or this month plus the eleven after it.

## What this article cannot tell you

We read the First Schedule, three service tax guides and one amendment order, all published by the Royal Malaysian Customs Department, on 27 November 2026. Four things remain outside that reading, and printing them as settled would be dishonest:

- **No single flat general threshold exists** in any document we reached. The FAQ explains how the threshold is calculated and never prints a general figure.
- **Several categories were not read at all** in this pass: accommodation, private clubs, logistics, telecommunications, parking, construction, private education, private healthcare, courier, cleaning, employment services, security and digital services by foreign providers. Each has its own row, and several have their own guides.
- **We reconstructed the current column (3) values** from the 2018 Regulations plus two amendment orders rather than from one consolidated text, because the department's index lists amendments without a consolidated First Schedule. That is the weakest link in this article, and it is why every figure above names the document it came from.
- **Two guides say "prescribed threshold" and never print a number**, which is how a reader ends up quoting a blog instead.

## Find your row before you find a number

The practical instruction is one line: identify your Group and item in the First Schedule, read column (3), then check whether an amendment order has replaced that figure since 2018. If your sector's guide prints an example rather than a statement, use the example to confirm you are reading the right row.

A single number repeated across the internet cannot do that job, because the number is not the same for every business. RM500,000 is right for a software consultancy and wrong for a restaurant by a factor of three.
