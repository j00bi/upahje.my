# POS: RM122 a month, and no free tier files e-invoice

A POS subscription in Malaysia starts at RM122 a month on StoreHub and runs to RM235 for the next tier, while Qashier charges RM8 a day for the platform plus RM1 to RM3 a day per terminal. None of the free POS tiers is documented as MyInvois-compliant; the RM0 plan that does document LHDN e-Invoice is Bukku, and it is accounting software, not a till.

## The published prices, as printed

StoreHub's Malaysian page prints Starter from RM122 a month and Advanced from RM235. Qashier prices by the day rather than the month, and offers a 24-month route at RM4,580 once.

That difference matters when you compare a monthly card against a daily one, so the table normalises nothing and shows each vendor's own unit.

| Vendor and plan | Price as printed | Terminal hardware | LHDN e-invoice on the pricing page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| StoreHub Starter | From RM122/month | no hardware price printed | not stated |
| StoreHub Advanced | From RM235/month | no hardware price printed | not stated |
| Qashier, software only | RM8/day | your own phone, tablet or terminal | not stated |
| Qashier with a terminal | RM8 + RM1 (XS) / RM2 (X2) / RM3 (XL SLIM) a day | included, nothing upfront | not stated |
| Qashier, paid upfront | RM4,580 once for 24 months, save RM1,260 | platform only | not stated |
| Bukku Launch, accounting not POS | RM0/month, free forever | not a till | yes, it supports LHDN e-Invoice |

The last column is deliberately empty for two of the three vendors: neither StoreHub nor Qashier states e-invoice support on the pricing page read here, so nothing is claimed for them. Qashier's prices also exclude SST, which the page prints.

## What the subscription does not include

The two vendors handle hardware in opposite ways. StoreHub's pricing page carries no hardware figures at all, so a terminal, a drawer and a receipt printer arrive as separate lines in a quote. Qashier folds the terminal into the daily rate: RM1 a day for the compact XS, RM2 for the X2, RM3 for the XL SLIM, nothing upfront, with the hardware owned after two years.

A shop that budgets RM122 for software and RM0 for hardware has budgeted half a till. Ask whether the quote is rent, purchase or lease, because the three end at different 24-month totals, and note that Qashier's daily figure carries on for as long as the shop keeps paying it.

## Why the POS e-invoice question is the expensive one

A till that is not wired to MyInvois leaves the filing to whoever does the books, and that is the manual retyping the whole exercise is meant to remove. A POS that is documented as compliant is worth a higher monthly fee than a cheaper till plus a worker typing invoices twice.

The free plan that does document e-Invoice is Bukku's Launch, and it is an accounting product with 100 MB of storage and 100 emails a month. It files e-invoices; it does not take payment at a counter or print a receipt, and that difference decides whether it is a solution for your shop or only for your bookkeeping.

## Do the daily prices add up to the monthly ones

Qashier at RM8 a day is about RM243 over a 30-day month before terminals, so the daily card is not automatically cheaper than StoreHub's RM122. It becomes cheaper when the shop opens fewer days than it pays for, which is a calendar question rather than a software one.

The RM4,580 for 24 months works out at about RM191 a month, which sits between the two StoreHub tiers and above Qashier's own daily rate at a full month.

## What to ask before signing

1. Is e-invoice compliance in the plan you quoted, in writing?
2. Does the price include the terminal, or is hardware separate?
3. What happens to the subscription if you close for a week?
4. Does the plan file to MyInvois directly, or export a file someone uploads?
5. What is the 24-month total, not the monthly headline?

Pay for the e-invoice route, not the till. A POS that takes money but leaves the invoice to a person is a counter with a subscription, and the subscription is the smaller cost of the two.
