## Sections

| H2 | why this section exists |
|----|-------------------------|
| Who publishes these numbers, and who does not? | States the publisher type and the unreadable platforms before any figure, so the table is never mistaken for a market rate |
| What does a published automation build cost? | The price rows, each with its own unit, publisher and date |
| What does an integration cost? | Names the missing per-integration price instead of manufacturing one, and shows what is published instead |
| Why do the same tasks carry different units? | Stops the reader comparing a session against a project |
| What changes the price of a quote? | The six factors the publishers themselves tier on |
| How do I compare two quotes when the units differ? | Converts both quotes to three comparable numbers |
| FAQ | One hourly figure, the missing marketplaces, and whether price implies quality. None answered in the body |
| Sources | The six publisher URLs, each with the date checked |
