Prepared 25 November 2026. Evidence: Google autocomplete pulled with `gl=my` on 25 November 2026, plus the live sitemap. Autocomplete proves a phrase is real and commonly typed. It does not prove volume — volume comes from Search Console after indexing.

## 1. Retitle the four drafts to the query

The titles are good lines and poor search targets. Match the query string in the title, H1 and first 100 words; keep the attitude in the standfirst.

## 3. Rules for every page

1. Exact query phrase in title, H1, first 100 words. No paraphrase of the head term.
2. A number in the title where a number exists. `RM3,448.50` beats any adjective.
3. Year-tag every page whose facts can change.
4. Answer in the first paragraph, expand after. Answer engines lift that block.
5. Exactly one table per page. Tables win snippets.
6. Internal links from the statutory pages into the commercial ones — the statutory pages will carry the traffic.
7. No keyword stuffing. One head term per URL, and let the body use natural language.

## 4. What we cannot do

We cannot manufacture volume. Autocomplete gives existence and shape; Search Console gives volume after indexing. Once pages are indexed, optimise titles by CTR rather than by guesswork — impressions with a low CTR is the signal that a title is failing, not that a topic is dead.
