## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Jurang yang wajar dibaca dua kali` for "the gap worth reading twice", keeping the aside but in BM word order.
2. `Itu bukan tafsiran: itulah yang dikatakan oleh dua baris pada halaman yang sama` restructures the English colon sentence into a BM one.
3. `Mesin menguruskan separuh kiri. Separuh kanan bergantung pada jam.` is a caption written as two BM sentences rather than a translation of the English caption.
4. `tool patut memerhati jam, bukan hanya penyerahan` for "the tool should watch the clock, not just the submission".
5. `itulah cara perniagaan mengeluarkan nota berbulan-bulan lewat` keeps the bluntness without a calque.
