No term here is legal or regulatory beyond the LHDN document names, which are quoted as printed. No DBP ruling is claimed. Each term is marked with its basis, and 'common Malaysian usage, no DBP coinage in use' applies to the loanwords.

## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Ikut satu pesanan` opens the mechanism section as an instruction rather than a translation of "Follow one order".
2. `Empat rekod bagi satu peristiwa` keeps the count and the bluntness.
3. `satu-satunya integrasi ialah manusia yang mengingati mana satu yang terkini` restructures "the only integration is a person remembering".
4. `Ia menelan kos dalam kerja semula dan bukan dalam lesen` turns "costs in rework rather than licences" into a BM verb phrase.
5. `Tulis juga` keeps the aside short; a literal "write it down anyway" would read formal.

## Register checks applied

- `anda` for the reader; `kami` where the page describes its own coverage.
- No particles, no `kalau`, no `macam mana`, no `yang mana`.
- `ialah` in definitional sentences only.
- No em dashes. No host paths, usernames or session identifiers.
- Quoted DOSM and KRI wording stays in English and is attributed in the sentence.

## For the native reader

- `pertubuhan` is DOSM's term and correct in a statistics sentence; `perniagaan` is plainer in the rest of the article, and both appear deliberately.
- `fragmentasi` is the loanword; `kepecahan sistem` is the purer BM form and reads less precisely for a technical reader.
