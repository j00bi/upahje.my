## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Susunan itu keluar daripada kiraan di bawah` avoids the calque `ranking itu datang daripada aritmetik` which would read translated.
2. `tuas terbesar pada bil bulanan` is a BM idiom for a lever, not a literal rendering of "the largest lever on a monthly bill".
3. `bukan dirata-ratakan kepada nilai tengah yang sopan` replaces "rather than averaging to a polite middle", which as a literal would have been stiff and unclear.
4. `yang menahannya pada 3.70` for "what holds it to 3.70"; the verb carries the clause instead of a noun phrase.
5. `resipi ulang semak` for "re-run recipe", a BM phrasing rather than `resipi untuk menjalankan semula`.

## For the native reader

- `kebolehlanjutan` is correct BM but heavy; `kebolehan dikembangkan` reads easier if house style prefers it, at the cost of precision.
- `jejak operasi` for operational footprint: understandable, but a reader may expect `saiz pemasangan`. Flagged rather than changed, because the criterion is about operational weight, not size alone.
