## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `RM100 membeli salah satu daripada dua perkara berbeza` avoids the literal `RM100 membeli satu daripada dua benda yang berbeza`, which reads translated.
2. `Ia membeli tempat duduk dalam aplikasi orang lain` keeps the seat metaphor but makes the ownership explicit, which the English leaves implicit.
3. `Kos meter yang sama kecil pada penggunaan ringan` restructures "the same meter cost is tiny at light use" into a BM subject-first clause.
4. `yang meninggalkan RM89 daripada RM100 tidak digunakan` replaces a noun-heavy calque of "leaves RM89 of the RM100 unspent".
5. `Kedua-dua garis itu bukan cadangan` for "Neither line is a recommendation"; `cadangan` is the BM word a buyer expects.
6. `Ringgit yang lebih kuat mengubah angka Claude dan membiarkan angka ChatGPT` restructures the English cause-and-effect sentence.

## For the native reader

- `tempat duduk` for a subscription seat is a direct loan translation. It reads clearly next to `meter`, but if house style prefers, `akaun pengguna` would be plainer at the cost of the metaphor.
- `tier` is kept because vendors and Malaysian resellers print it. `peringkat` is the BM alternative and makes the table headers longer.
