## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `penawaran yang diterbitkan, bukan keseluruhan pasaran` for "published supply, not the whole market"; the literal `bekalan yang diterbitkan` is the English noun order.
2. `Apa yang tidak dapat dibaca sama pentingnya` restructures "what could not be read matters as much".
3. `Jalur ini menerangkan lapisan penawaran tempatan` avoids the calque `band ini menggambarkan lapisan` which reads Indonesian.
4. `Mengikut projek, harga yang diterbitkan bermula dari` replaces "per project, published prices run from", which as a literal is malformed.
5. `Tulis jawapannya di sebelah angka daripada tarikh yang sama` restructures the long English comparison sentence.
6. `Ia satu angka yang diterbitkan daripada satu profil` for "it is one published figure from one profile"; the BM drops the copula.
