No term here is legal or regulatory beyond licence names, which are proper names quoted untranslated. No DBP ruling is claimed. Each term is marked with its basis: a DBP or established Malaysian form where one is in use, and 'common Malaysian usage, no DBP coinage in use' where the English loanword was kept.

## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Lesen ialah perbezaan yang menentukan sama ada anda boleh menggunakan sesuatu model dalam perniagaan` restructures the English opening; a literal `Lesen adalah perbezaan` would break the copula rule.
2. `MIT ialah yang longgar` avoids the calque `MIT adalah yang permisif`.
3. `Saiz ialah tembok pertama` keeps the metaphor but uses the BM predicate form.
4. `Jadi artikel ini tidak boleh memberitahu berapa kos hosting sendiri` turns "so this article cannot tell you what self-hosting costs" into a BM construction without a dummy subject.
5. `membeli-belah hos dan bukan model` for "shop the host rather than the model", a natural BM reduplication.
6. `ia tidak berpura-pura menarik garis tersebut` for "it does not pretend to draw that line", keeping the bluntness.

## Register checks applied

- `anda` for the reader; `saya` only where the page states its own limits.
- No particles, no `kalau`, no `macam mana`, no `yang mana`.
- `ialah` in definitional sentences only.
- No em dashes. No host paths, usernames or session identifiers.
- Every price, parameter count and licence name identical to the English edition and to Table 6.

## For the native reader

- `kad model` for model card is plain and understood; `kad maklumat model` is the longer alternative.
- `luar puncak` for off-peak follows Malaysian electricity-tariff usage, which is the closest familiar analogue.
