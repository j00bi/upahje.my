## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Unitnya bukan perbualan dan bukan sesi` restructures "the unit is not a conversation and not a session".
2. `Bentuk praktikalnya:` opens the worked sequence; the English "The practical shape" would read stiff as a calque.
3. `jam yang bermula apabila pelanggan menghantar mesej` explains the window rather than translating "clock" literally.
4. `ia menandakan bahawa dua halaman menerangkan mesej yang sama secara berbeza` restructures the closing sentence of the conflict section.
5. `Tiada laluan sandbox percuma yang didokumenkan` states the gap as a limitation, not as a policy.

## For the native reader

- `dihantar kepada penerima` is longer than `dihantar`, and it is deliberate: the charge turns on delivery, and the shorter word loses that.
- `templat` is the standard BM loanword; `acuan` would be read as a mould.
