## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Vendor menulis "agent harness" atau ringkasnya "harness"` avoids the calque `Vendor mengatakan`.
2. `Kedua-duanya tidak menganggapnya sebagai kiasan` restructures "Neither treats it as a metaphor" into a BM subject-first sentence.
3. `Empat perkara, dan setiap satu satu baris, bukan penerangan penuh` keeps the compressed English and reads naturally in BM.
4. `frasa "AI harness" tidak muncul` for "the phrase does not appear"; `muncul` is what a Malaysian writer uses here rather than `hadir`.
5. `label itu hiasan` for "the label is decoration"; a short BM predicate instead of `adalah hiasan`.
