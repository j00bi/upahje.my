## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Harness menyimpan kunci anda di luar perbualan` restructures "keeps your key outside the conversation", which as a literal would use a noun heavy `di luar daripada perbualan`.
2. `sebab itulah ia boleh disandarkan` replaces "which is why it can be backed up", with the passive restructured.
3. `Kunci itu kemudian tinggal dalam transkrip` for "the key then lives in a transcript"; `tinggal` is the natural BM verb rather than `hidup`.
4. `Geledah sejarah repositori` for "search your repository history", a verb a Malaysian writer would choose over `cari`.
5. `Kebocoran yang puncanya tidak dikenal pasti akan berulang` avoids the calque `kebocoran tanpa punca yang dikenal pasti`.
6. `Pasukan yang memutar dahulu dan membatalkan kemudian` mirrors the English but keeps BM word order for the agent phrase.
