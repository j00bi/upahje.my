## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Ia memberitahu apa yang klien bajetkan secara awam` restructures "it tells you what clients publicly budget"; the literal would start with a dummy subject.
2. `Asas itu lebih sempit daripada bunyinya` for "that is a narrower base than it sounds", a BM comparative that reads naturally.
3. `Halangannya bukan kemahiran` for "the barrier is not skill"; `halangan` is the BM noun a Malaysian writer uses here.
4. `Yang paling rendah RM150` rather than `Kadar yang paling rendah ialah RM150`; BM drops the copula in list leads.
5. `bersaing dengan sesiapa yang ada pelayar` for "competes with anyone with a browser", restructured to avoid a noun stack.
6. `Anggaplah ia langkah pertama` keeps the imperative shape the English uses, without a `-lah` particle elsewhere in the article.

## Register checks applied

- `anda` for the reader; `kami` where the page estimates.
- No particles except the single `Anggaplah` imperative, which is a verb form rather than a discourse particle.
- No `kalau`, no `macam mana`; `jika` and `bagaimana` only.
- `ialah` in definitional sentences only.
- No em dashes. No host paths, usernames or session identifiers.
- Every rate and posting month identical to the English edition and to Table 3.
