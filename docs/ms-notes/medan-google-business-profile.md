## Sentences written fresh rather than translated

1. `Get those wrong and the profile can be restricted` is carried as `Salah pada tiga perkara itu, profil boleh dihadkan`, a shorter BM construction.
2. `Waktu yang salah menghasilkan ulasan bintang satu yang berbunyi "tutup sedangkan kata buka"` rewrites the English example rather than translating it word for word.
3. `Di sinilah "ada parkir" dan "boleh masuk kerusi roda" diletakkan` uses the phrases a Malaysian reader actually sees on a listing.
4. `Ayat itu sebabnya pengesahan mesti kekal semasa` restructures "that sentence is the whole reason".
5. `Yang menggigit ialah...` keeps the idiomatic warning without a label.

## Register checks applied

- `anda` for the reader; `kami` where the page states its own limits.
- No particles, no `kalau`, no `macam mana`, no `yang mana`.
- `ialah` in definitional sentences only.
- No em dashes. No host paths, usernames or session identifiers.
- Quoted Google wording stays in English and is attributed in the sentence.

## For the native reader

- `kedai` for a storefront is right for a shop; for an office or a workshop counter, `premis` reads better and should be chosen per article.
- `penggantungan` is correct but formal; `profil kena gantung` is what an owner would say. The article uses the formal term once and then explains it in plain words.
